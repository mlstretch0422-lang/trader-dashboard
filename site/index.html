The project behind the product

Built around Mason’s real trading development — not a finished-guru story.

The point of Signal Bridge is to make the full process usable and reviewable: what was believed, what was coded, what was tested, what failed, what happened in the market, what was journaled, and what changed next. The website, Discord, Pine tools, and research record are meant to become one connected system over time.

System Loop

The product should learn from the trading.

Evidence Discipline

No fake certainty.

A working indicator, a Discord alert, or one profitable configuration can be useful without being proof that the strategy or every internal filter has edge.

Verified

Accepted project rule

Governance or an explicitly accepted operating constraint. This label alone does not mean statistical proof.

Testing

Under evaluation

Implemented or observed, but still requires controlled evidence before promotion.

Untested

Hypothesis / module

Interesting idea or coded component without sufficient isolated evidence yet.

Retired

Negative evidence preserved

Failed or superseded ideas stay visible so the project does not relearn the same lesson twice.
