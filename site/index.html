The project behind the product

Built around Mason’s real trading development — not a finished-guru story.

The point of Signal Bridge is to make the full process usable and reviewable: what was believed, what was coded, what was tested, what failed, what happened in the market, what was journaled, and what changed next. The website, Discord, Pine tools, and research record are meant to become one connected system over time.
