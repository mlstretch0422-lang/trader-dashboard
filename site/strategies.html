Liquidity + ORB continuation framework

Map Asia, London, and previous-day structure; build a local ORB; read pre-New-York behavior; wait for acceptance, rejection, or a retest; then participate only when the price-action case and liquidity objective line up. The current research job is to isolate which measurable pieces deserve to survive.
