Research Pipeline

How a strategy earns its way forward.

Signal Bridge treats strategy development like a pipeline instead of a binary “works / does not work” label.

01 · Raw processCapture how the trade is actually seen and executed.
02 · Mechanical definitionTranslate objective pieces into precise rules.
03 · ImplementationCode the rule without pretending code equals edge.
04 · AttributionIsolate clocks, filters, entries, stops, and exits.
05 · Forward evidenceReplay, paper, and walk-forward behavior under version control.
06 · Live evidenceOnly real live history can support a live-performance claim.
Library Rules

The labels mean something.

Member-facing status describes where an idea sits in the project. It does not let a convenient word such as “verified” silently become “statistically proven.”

Verified

Accepted project rule

An explicitly accepted operating or governance rule. Statistical evidence must still be stated separately.

Testing

Under evaluation

Coded, observed, or historically profitable does not automatically mean isolated edge.

Untested

Hypothesis

A useful idea or module that has not yet earned controlled evidence.

Retired

Negative evidence kept

Failed and superseded versions remain part of the research record.
