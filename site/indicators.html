Indicator Standard

Every future tool should answer four questions.

Purpose

What job does it do?

Visualization, alerting, research, execution support, or strategy testing should be explicit.

Version

What exact logic?

Clock, entry definition, filters, risk, exits, and defaults need a reproducible version.

Evidence

What has actually been tested?

Implementation, backtest, attribution, walk-forward, paper-forward, and live are separate stages.

Provenance

Where did it come from?

Every promoted rule should trace back to source material and preserved research decisions.
