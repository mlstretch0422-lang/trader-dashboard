The Process

How the morning is actually read.

The ORB is a reference inside a larger market map. The model starts with overnight structure and finishes with a confirmed response at a level — not with “box broke, click buy.”

01Map Asia, London, previous-day liquidity
02Build ORB high, low, midpoint, range
03Read 8:15–9:30 sweeps, breaks, acceptance
04Watch the NY-open participation shift
05Wait for retest / reclaim / rejection
06Confirm context without replacing the trigger
07Rotate toward the next liquidity objective
Setup Anatomy

Three pictures that actually teach the model.

These diagrams replace the old random archive screenshots. They show the repeatable shapes the strategy is trying to recognize; real chart captures can later be paired beside them as reviewed case studies.

Long ideaBreak → retest → reclaim

Price proves it can hold beyond the range, returns to a mapped level, then reclaims/defends it. Context can strengthen permission, but the retest response is the trigger family.

Reversal ideaSweep → fail → reclaim

Price runs an obvious high/low, cannot accept beyond it, and reclaims back through the reference. The sweep is not enough by itself — failure and confirmation are what make it interesting.

PassDouble-sided mess / no confirmation

If price keeps crossing midpoint, breaking both sides, or never confirms after the intended location, there is no reason to manufacture a trade. The no-trade outcome is useful session data too.

Confluence Stack

Permission versus trigger.

The beginner-friendly rule from the project source is simple: confluences give permission; the actual trigger is still price reacting at the intended level.

1 · LocationORB edge / midpoint · session high/low · prior level · FVG / liquidity area

Start with a meaningful place, not an indicator crossing in the middle of nowhere.

2 · Bias4H / 1H context · session structure · ES/NQ relationship

Decide whether the larger market is helping, fighting, or neutral toward the idea.

3 · ContextVWAP · EMA · volume / participation · news risk

These help judge environment quality; none replaces price confirmation.

4 · TriggerReclaim · reject · engulf · wick rejection · displacement / MSS

This is the actual price response that turns a watched level into an actionable setup.

5 · InvalidationAcceptance through the level / stop boundary

If the thesis fails, the trade is wrong. No narrative rescue.
