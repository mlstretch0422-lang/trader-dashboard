The Model

The decision process in one pass.

The raw decision-process source makes one thing clear: the ORB is a reference inside a larger market map, not the whole strategy and not an automatic breakout signal.

01Map Asia, London, previous-day structure
02Build the local ORB high, low, midpoint
03Read 8:15–9:30 behavior and first breaks
04Evaluate the New York open and participation
05Wait for retest, hold, reclaim, or rejection
06Stack contextual evidence instead of one trigger
07Rotate toward the next plausible liquidity target
Core mental model: “Where is price trying to go, what structure has it accepted or rejected, and is the retest giving enough evidence to participate?”
Evidence State

What is measurable, implemented, observed, or still discretionary.

These labels deliberately stop short of saying “proven edge” where the repository does not contain an isolated controlled test.

Session highs / lowsObjective

Asia, London, and previous-day references are measurable levels. Their usefulness as directional targets still depends on context and has not been isolated as an edge contribution.

ORB high / low / midpointTesting

Implemented across multiple historical versions and backtests, but the exact clock differs across sources. The level itself is objective; the preferred clock is not settled.

Retest / acceptance entryTesting

Central to Mason’s raw process and present in historical strategy variants. Existing evidence does not cleanly isolate retest versus immediate breakout on the same data.

VWAP alignmentTesting

Used as a directional filter in the process and implemented in code. The repository explicitly notes that no clean VWAP-on versus VWAP-off attribution test exists.

EMA trend alignmentUntested

Secondary confirmation in the raw process. It is implemented in historical code, but its independent contribution remains unknown.

FVG / imbalance contextUntested

Part of the discretionary framework and visible in historical trade notes, but not established as a required mechanical rule with isolated performance evidence.

Volume / participation readingDiscretionary

Volume is measurable, but interpreting conviction, participation, or weak acceptance remains contextual and visually judged in the current process.

Liquidity target selectionDiscretionary

The candidate levels are objective. Choosing which target price is actually seeking remains one of the most discretionary parts of the framework.

Known Conflict

The ORB clock is not settled.

This is exactly the kind of contradiction Signal Bridge is supposed to preserve instead of hiding.

Raw decision process + current alert integration8:00–8:15 ET

The actual-process document uses the 8:00–8:15 range, and the current v1.1 integration indicator follows that clock.

Simplified strategy source-of-truth v19:30–9:45 ET

The older mechanical specification calls the cash-open range primary and explicitly questions the pre-market clock.

Current research position: neither document gets to erase the other. The audit identifies the clock mismatch as a major confounder because changing the ORB window changes the market regime, entry timing, range distribution, stops, and targets. The clocks need like-for-like isolation testing.

Research Queue

What has to happen before promotion.

The page should get stronger because the evidence gets stronger — not because the copy gets more confident.

ORB clock isolation

Run comparable 8:00–8:15 and cash-open range definitions on matched dates, cost models, timeframe, sizing, and exits.

Entry-mode attribution

Separate immediate breakout, boundary retest, and midpoint retest so a profitable full configuration is not mistaken for proof of one entry method.

Filter attribution

Test VWAP, EMA, displacement, range, FVG, and other filters one variable at a time or through a controlled experiment matrix.

Risk-model reconciliation

Lock position sizing, stop placement, target method, commission, slippage, and force-flat behavior before comparing strategy variants.

Journal data structure

Turn screenshots and notes into trade-level records with setup type, version, timestamps, result, MAE/MFE, context, and review notes.

Forward evidence

Only after historical attribution: replay / walk-forward, paper-forward, funded or live evidence kept in separate stages.
