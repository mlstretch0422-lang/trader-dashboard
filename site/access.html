How login works

Three steps. No second account to remember.

The login link is intentionally generated inside Discord so the private workspace can be attached to the same identity that owns your journal records.

01

Open the Discord

Enter the Signal Bridge server on the Discord account you want tied to your trading records.

02

Run /member-login

The Signal Bridge app replies privately with a one-time sign-in link. The link expires after ten minutes.

03

Open the workspace

Tap the link. The browser receives a secure member session and opens your private workspace, journal, and Strategy Lab.
