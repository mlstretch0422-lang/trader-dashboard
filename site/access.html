How login works

Three steps. No second account to remember.

The login link is generated inside Discord so the private workspace is attached to the same identity that owns the journal and member Strategy DNA.

01

Open the Discord

Enter the Signal Bridge server on the Discord account you want tied to your trading records.

02

Run /member-login

The Signal Bridge app replies privately with a one-time sign-in link. The link expires after ten minutes.

03

Open the private OS

Tap the link. A secure browser session opens the member dashboard, Live Desk, journal, Strategy Lab, and Indicator Workspace.
