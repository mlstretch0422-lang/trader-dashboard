Capture Architecture

Write once. Reuse everywhere.

The live workflow is intentionally simple enough to fit real trading: journal in Discord, preserve the raw note privately, normalize it later, connect it to Signal Bridge events, and only publish educational material after review.

01Discord journal postFast, short-form note while the trade is fresh.
02Private captureSigned Discord interaction stores the raw note and provenance in D1.
03NormalizeSymbol, direction, setup, result, tags, screenshots, and signal link become structured fields.
04ReviewSeparate what actually happened from hindsight and research interpretation.
05Publish / researchApproved lessons can feed education, strategy evidence, and future testing.
Private-first by design. Discord captures default to PRIVATE + RAW. The current public beta page reads only entries explicitly marked PUBLISHED, while the Worker restricts Discord capture to the configured application, server, and trade-journal channel.
