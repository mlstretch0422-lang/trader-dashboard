## 🔍 Key Findings at a Glance

### Critical Contradictions Found

| Contradiction | Evidence | Implication |
|---------------|----------|-------------|
| **Retest vs. Breakout** | Breakout PF=2.03 vs. Retest PF=0.15 | Docs claim retest edge; data shows opposite |
| **Stop-Loss Underperformance** | Stop exits: -$2,639 (PF=0.07) vs. Limit/Market: +$4,545 (PF~14) | Current SL placement is a net drag |
| **One-Trade-Per-Day Not Enforced** | 11 of 17 days show 2+ trades | Rule documented but not applied to paper data |

### Evidence-Based Metrics

**From 38 reconstructed trades**:
- **Overall**: PF=1.78, net=$2,381, expectancy=$62.66/trade ✓
- **Win rate**: 31.6% (acceptable for PF > 1.5)
- **Best exit type**: Limit/Market (expectancy $303/trade)
- **Worst exit type**: Stop-loss (expectancy -$120/trade)
