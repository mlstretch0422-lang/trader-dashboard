Trading Psychology Notes

A successful trader is made when you take money away from the aspect of trading. 

People overvalue the idea of profitability and trading instead of just focusing on the process and realizing that you are just reading charts and clicking a button based off of your setup. 

We perceive trading as a tool to make money as to where fundamentally it is just looking at lines on a chart drawing boxes and clicking a button. 

When you tie value to something is when you start becoming emotional. 

You must be a fully focused observer, and not think like you’re a real person outside of the charts. When on the charts you don’t want any outside distractions you're just observing the market. 

Your problem in trading always comes back to wanting more money. 

You're a creative artist, you are an artist looking at art trying to interpret it. 

Dumb down your idea of what trading is. Quit acting like its the greatest thing in the world just treat it like you sit there and read lines on a chart.

In your mind trading should be like waking up and drinking water. It should be boring, not some crazy cool idea it's just a job.

The first step to reprogramming yourself as a trader is to dumb down the idea of trading. Nothing is cool about this. I sit here, look at a chart and click a button. 

Second thing we must understand is money has nothing to do with the act of trading. The actual act of what the market is has nothing to do with money so don’t think about it when on the charts.

When you tie the aspect of money takes all the value away from what the market actually is.

Process orientation is the next step. 

You will start being the most successful when you start doing less, you can’t brute force your way to profitability. Because 95% of trading is doing nothing. 

Don’t bring outside things into trading because then you stop paying attention to the data and you start thinking about ways the button can affect you outside of the charts.

Work could be nothing sitting off the charts could be work because you're actively reserving capital instead of sitting on the chart overtrading. 

Good habits are the only thing that will make you successful long term. 

Trading is not about making money its about keeping your money. 

Habits: Not over trading, Only Trade your system, Don’t over leverage, Being disciplined, patient, knowing when to step away, asking yourself hard questions like what did you do wrong today, quit sweeping things under the wrong saying you won’t make that mistake again. 

Be in a submissive state, be okay with growing, don't act like you're always right, just submit to the journey and process and learn from mistakes. 

Your journey is for you and it needs to happen for growth.

Success for me should be going into a day being very patient taking my setup and whether i lose or win we walk away okay with what happened because we did what we were supposed to. 

Program yourself to be satisfied building good habits instead of making money. 

You must program yourself from not making money but building good habits. 

The outcome is what we tie to what success looks like. The outcome isn't the success because the work is what got that. 

Your process is what's going to actually make you money, learn to be so disconnected from money, think that there isn’t even money involved on the market just execute your process. 

I made this mistake because the market is showing me exactly what's wrong with my process. 

You think that losing money is doing something wrong and winning money is something right. But that's not true because its all about your process. 

If you go into the market and have a green day not using your edge you need to be able to look at yourself and say it was a bad day and this isn’t my money because the money doesn’t matter that's a loss because you didn’t follow your rules and your process. 

You need to teach yourself that Red is not wrong, Green is not right. Are you building good habits is what the real value is and you will start to see success. 

Number 1 trading is a different type of work. You will be the most successful when you feel like you're doing the least amount of work because you are submitting to the process.

Number 2 View the market as nothing more than viewing lines on a chart you're clicking a button based off of data nothing more nothing less quit over sexualising and fantasising about trading.

Number three everything that you do with your trading every mistake and failure you make comes back to the idea that you're more worried about money then the actual process of what trading is. Money is a outcome stop focusing on making money disconnect it from your head build a system and build confidence with that system come into the market and focus on building good habits.

Number 4 is to build good habits. Your main approach should be to learn to be focused and learn satisfaction from building good habits. Your only goal should be to focus on building habits completely disconnect from passing an account the future, your in the moment purely there looking at a monitor with buttons and lines and you press a button that's it there is no idea of money anywhere and you apply risk and you apply your system and you execute and you walk away nothing cool nothing fancy don't over value trading cause when you do it brings all the negative emotion in. 

Reprogramming yourself is very important cause your idea of what trading is will impact everything you do if its some big crazy idea thats so far out of reach then it will be so far out of reach the idea of profitability is so far out of reach because your grasping for profitability you're trading as the version of you that is TRYING TO BE profitable not the version of you THAT IS profitable.  Because you set up this foundation in your head that trading and profitability is so far out of reach and over valued that you're constantly trying to grab it instead of just being it. You have to submit to the process, you have to fail, you have to learn, you have to fall on your ass a couple of times and pick yourself back up. 

It's not just all strategy it's not all risk management its about understanding yourself it's a game of you vs you nobody else. You have to have a deep self understanding of your awareness, perception, your reality, the way that you view and operate in certain environments.
